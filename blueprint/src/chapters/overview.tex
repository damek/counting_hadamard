\chapter{Overview}\label{chap:overview}

This blueprint maps the Lean formalization of
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.30013}{arXiv:2603.30013},
which gives a first-order asymptotic expansion for partial Hadamard
matrices in the cubic regime $t\ge C_0 n^3$.
Every labeled result in the paper is formalized without
\texttt{sorry} or internal \texttt{axiom}.
